Thinking Framework


Rise to the Challenge

Decentralized technology, governance, startups and poker appear unrelated but they are all systems of distributed actors operating under uncertainty. I like to think of them under the lens of: systems, strategy, and people.

01

Systems

How do distributed components behave collectively, and what emerges from structure? Governance, markets, and organizations run on incentives — read them well and the efficiencies, durability, and leverage become visible.

02

Strategy

How do you choose and commit under incomplete information? Positioning, timing, and trade-offs — the same discipline applies to protocol design, business development, governance, and the poker table.

03

People

Systems are built by people and interact with people. Understanding how humans reason, coordinate, compete, and cooperate under uncertainty is the core operating layer.
